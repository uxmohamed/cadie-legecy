We've completely overhauled keyboard navigation in Caddy to help you stay in the flow. You can now perform almost every action without lifting your hands from the keyboard.

**Global Shortcuts** give you instant access to key features from anywhere in the app. Press `?` to view the new help menu, or use `c` and `/` to instantly focus the capture input.

**Vim-style Navigation** brings the speed of power-user tools to your link list. Use `j` and `k` to move up and down through your links, and `Enter` to open them. No mouse required.

**Quick Actions** let you manage your library with single keystrokes. Press `e` to edit a link, `d` to delete it, or `Backspace` to archive. You can even select multiple links and perform batch actions.

To see all available shortcuts, just press `?` at any time.

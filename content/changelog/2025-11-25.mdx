We've completely overhauled keyboard navigation and organization in Cadie to help you stay in the flow. You can now perform almost every action without lifting your hands from the keyboard, and manage your links with dedicated Archive and Recycle Bin views.

**Global Shortcuts** give you instant access to key features from anywhere in the app. Press `?` to view the new help menu (hint: there's now a badge above the input to remind you!), or use `c` and `/` to instantly focus the capture input. Toggle the sidebar with `[` to maximize your workspace.

**Vim-style Navigation** brings the speed of power-user tools to your link list. Use `j` and `k` to move up and down through your links, and `Enter` to open them. No mouse required.

**Quick Actions** let you manage your library with single keystrokes. Press `e` to edit a link, `d` to move it to the Recycle Bin, or `q` to archive it. Use `Ctrl+A` (or `Cmd+A`) to select all links for batch operations.

**Archive & Recycle Bin** views are now available in the sidebar. Archive your links to keep them out of sight but accessible, or move them to the Recycle Bin for soft deletion. All deleted items can be reviewed before permanent removal.

To see all available shortcuts, just press `?` at any time.

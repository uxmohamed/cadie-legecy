### One-click capture

Save the current page with one click from the browser toolbar. It's the fastest way to add anything to your library without leaving your current tab.

### Keyboard shortcut

Use `⌥ + D` (Mac) or `Alt + D` (Windows/Linux) to save without using the mouse. Designed for speed and minimal interruption to your workflow.

### Context menu

Right-click on any page, link, or image to save it instantly. Precision saving exactly where you need it.

Links saved through the extension appear immediately in Cadie. No setup or extra steps required.

changeType: added

We've added automatic URL cleaning and enhanced metadata extraction to make your saved links more useful.

When you save a link, Cadie now automatically removes tracking parameters and extracts rich metadata including titles, descriptions, and Open Graph images. This keeps your links clean and provides better context at a glance.

**Features:**
- Automatic tracking parameter removal
- Enhanced metadata extraction
- Favicon and image previews
- Improved link previews in your dashboard
- Better organization with extracted data

Your saved links are now cleaner and more informative, making it easier to find and reference content later.

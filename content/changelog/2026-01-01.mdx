Welcome to Cadie

Cadie starts with a simple belief: links are part of your thinking, and they deserve a place that feels stable, private, and easy to return to. This first release lays the foundation for a personal library for the web.

At its core, Cadie is a secure space to save and organize links without friction or noise.

![Cadie Interface](/images/changelog/launch.png)

### Save without effort

Capture links directly from the dashboard. A browser extension is coming soon to make saving even easier.

### Secured by default

Everything you save is stored securely. Your links aren’t exposed or shared — they live in a private space you can rely on.

### Stay organized, quietly

Links are cleaned automatically, with tracking removed and metadata extracted. Archive what you want to keep out of sight, or move items to the Recycle Bin when you’re done.

### Keyboard-first

Navigate your entire library using the keyboard. Press `?` to see all shortcuts.

This is just the beginning. We’re building Cadie carefully, one step at a time. Thanks for being early — your feedback will shape what comes next.

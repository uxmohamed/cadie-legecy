Welcome to Cadie! 🎉

This is the very first release of Cadie, a simple tool to save and organize links from across the web. Think of it as your personal library for the internet.

**Save anything** — Capture links directly from the dashboard. A Chrome extension is on the way to make saving even easier.

**Stay organized** — Your links are automatically cleaned up, with tracking parameters removed and metadata extracted. Archive what you want to keep but hide, or move things to the Recycle Bin.

**Keyboard-first** — Navigate your entire library without touching the mouse. Press `?` to see all available shortcuts.

This is just the beginning. We're starting small and building from here. Thank you for being one of the first to try Cadie — your feedback will shape what comes next.

Happy saving!

### Spaces

This release introduces Spaces — a new way to organize your links in Cadie.

Spaces let you group links in a way that matches how you think. You can create spaces for projects, topics, or anything else you’re working on, and move between them easily as your library grows.

They’re meant to stay out of the way. No setup to get started, no rules to follow — just places to keep related links together.

![Spaces Interface](/images/changelog/spaces-update.png)

### A smoother experience

Alongside Spaces, we made a set of behind-the-scenes improvements to make Cadie feel more responsive and reliable as you move around the app. Things should load faster, updates feel more immediate, and transitions stay smooth.

You won’t see most of this — and that’s the point.

### Mobile polish

We also spent time refining the mobile experience:

- Layouts feel more balanced on smaller screens
- Menus are easier to tap and move through
- Settings and dialogs behave more consistently

Small adjustments, but they add up to a better experience on mobile.
